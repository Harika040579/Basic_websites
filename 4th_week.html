INDIAN CRICKETERS

VIRAT KOHLI, also known as "King Kohli," is a legendary cricketer with 82 centuries. He was one of India's best captains and is known as the "Chase Master." I am a big fan of his game!

ROHIT SHARMA (born 30 April 1987) is India's captain in Test and ODI formats. He is regarded as one of the greatest opening batters and is famous for his ability to hit sixes.

HARDIK PANDYA (born 11 October 1993) is an Indian international all-rounder. He is a right-handed middle-order batsman and a fast-medium bowler.
