Soft drinks

Soft drinks are non-alcoholic, carbonated beverages that typically contain water, sweeteners (such as sugar or artificial substitutes), flavorings, and sometimes caffeine. They come in various flavors, including cola, citrus, and fruit-based varieties. Some popular soft drink brands include Coca-Cola, Pepsi, Sprite, and Fanta.
